AI Ethics

According to UNESCO, the four core values of AI ethics are...

1) Human rights and human dignity Respect, protection and promotion of human rights and fundamental freedoms and human dignity
2) Peace Living in peaceful, just, and interconnected societies
3)Accessibility Ensuring diversity and inclusiveness
4) Sustainability Environment and ecosystem flourishing

References:

UNESCO. (n.d.). Recommendation on the ethics of artificial intelligence. https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence/recommendation-ethics
